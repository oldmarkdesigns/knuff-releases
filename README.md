# Knuff for Mac

Downloads for [Knuff](https://knuff-website.vercel.app/): research Swedish companies where your work could help, draft a short first email, and send it from your own Gmail once you approve it.

**[Download the latest version for Mac (Apple Silicon)](https://github.com/oldmarkdesigns/knuff-releases/releases/latest/download/Knuff-mac-arm64.dmg)**

## Install

1. Open `Knuff-mac-arm64.dmg` and drag **Knuff** to **Applications**.
2. Open Knuff. The first time, macOS says it can't verify the app, because Knuff isn't notarised by Apple yet.
3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to Knuff, then confirm.

Knuff then walks you through setup: your studio, the AI that does the research (Claude Code with your own Claude plan), and your Gmail.

## Requirements

- A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later).
- A Claude Pro or Max plan for research, or an Anthropic or OpenAI API key.
- A Gmail account to send from.

Your data stays on your Mac, in `~/Library/Application Support/Knuff`.
